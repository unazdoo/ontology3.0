# Map Resource Sources

- `china-provinces.geojson`: public administrative boundary geometry fetched from `https://geo.datav.aliyun.com/areas_v3/bound/100000_full.json` on 2026-09-07. Display attribution: DataV GeoAtlas. Retained locally for offline prototype rendering; not a production map-service authorization.
- Enterprise and bank points: user-authorized synthetic city-level demonstration placements, EPSG:4326 longitude/latitude. Not verified legal addresses, project sites, or observed operating locations.
- Existing investment observation points remain separate from enterprise master data.

The prototype must keep synthetic point provenance visible and must not infer legal identity, business relationships, or financial risk from geographical proximity.
